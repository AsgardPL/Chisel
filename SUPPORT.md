# Support

Chisel is a personal project, developed and maintained by me primarily for my own projects. It's free and open source, and it's provided as-is.

## What to expect

- There is **no guaranteed support**. Issues and questions are read when time allows, but there's no promise of a response, a fix, or a timeline.
- Issues may be closed without a detailed explanation if they're out of scope, can't be reproduced, or aren't something I plan to address.
- Pull requests are not welcome. Chisel is not an open-contribution engine. If I see a fork with an interesting feature, I may reach out, but generally Chisel isn't meant for community driven development.

## Where to go

- **Found a bug?** Open an issue using the bug report form, and include as much detail as you can. Clear steps to reproduce make a huge difference.
- **Have a question or need help?** Visit the Wiki. Someone may have made a guide for just this!

Thanks for using Chisel, and for understanding!
